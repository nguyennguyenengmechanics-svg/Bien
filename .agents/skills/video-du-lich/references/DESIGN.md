## Style prompt
Personal Vietnamese travel journal. Real footage drives the story. Warm cream rounded translucent panels, jade headings, deep ink body text and restrained terracotta emphasis. Modern Be Vietnam Pro throughout. Calm entrances and wipes. Supplemental information remains secondary. The v3 decision supersedes earlier exploratory v1/v2 designs and the later v4 experiment for this skill.

## Palette and typography
- #FCFAF5: cream surfaces and non-filmed editorial backgrounds.
- #17353B: dark ink.
- #31746A: jade labels, active pill and progress.
- #245F55: heading emphasis.
- #A84936: caption emphasis.
- #EDF2ED: quiet supporting cards.
- #FFFFFF: photo borders and small informational card.
- Be Vietnam Pro Regular400, Medium500, SemiBold600, Bold700. Local fonts and OFL are bundled. Heading60px, captions35px, metadata28px at1080×1920; shorten or split text before shrinking.

## Composition
Same v3 CSS is bundled as assets/template/theme.css. Scene-content fills the frame with flex and padding. Upper small brand/route, concise panel heading, clear footage area, optional lower chip, caption card and quiet footer. No mandatory extra chip if it only repeats the heading. Avoid faces, actual signs and salient travel details when placing panels. Procedure and missing-footage cards use cream; never fabricate filmed footage for them. Photos preserve their aspect and orientation, with redactions already baked in.

## Motion
GSAP paused timeline. Typical header entrance0.5–0.7s, restrained30px movement, chapter wipe0.44s. Stagger items only to explain order. Outgoing content stays present until the transition. Caption fade around0.13s plus a hard opacity reset; gaps prevent overlap. No compulsory beat montage or extra-fast opener from v4; a stronger opener can be proposed when a new request calls for it.

## Reference facts, not reusable trip defaults
The approved Narita v3 story uses part1 then part2, 00:25 VietnamUTC+7 departure and08:00 JapanUTC+9 arrival. Two sign groups are “Arrivals, Domestic” and “International Transfer”. This itinerary follows the former for entry. Story sequence: entry inspection → baggage → customs → pickup; unfilmed stages are labelled. Visit Japan Web is a small auxiliary card in the entry section, not the main subject. A provided friends photo closes that first internship day. Reconfirm or replace these particulars for new footage; they are not global templates for all journeys.
